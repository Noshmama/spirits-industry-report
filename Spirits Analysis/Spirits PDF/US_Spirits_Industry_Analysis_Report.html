1. Executive Summary

  • Total Market: The U.S. spirits market grew from 145M to 243M 9L cases (2003-2021), then contracted to 227M cases by 2024 (-7% from peak).
  • Biggest Winner: Tequila/Mezcal grew 302% (8M to 32M cases), gaining 8.6 percentage points of market share.
  • Biggest Loser: Rum peaked in 2013 and has declined 19%, losing 4.3pp of market share.
  • Vodka Maturation: Still the largest category at 74M cases (33% share), but peaked in 2021 and showing signs of decline.
  • Whiskey Renaissance: American Whiskey (+126%) and Irish Whiskey (+884%) drove category growth.
  • 2025-2029 Outlook: Total market expected to range from -5% (aggressive) to +1% (dampened), with Tequila continuing to outperform.

5. Key Structural Shifts

1. Tequila Dominance

Tequila/Mezcal has become the 3rd largest spirits category, overtaking Rum (2019) and Canadian Whisky (2015). With continued momentum, it could challenge American Whiskey for the #2 position within 3-5 years.

2. Vodka Maturation

After decades of growth, Vodka peaked at 78.1M cases in 2021 and has declined 5.1% since. The category remains dominant but is shifting focus from volume to premiumization.

3. Whiskey Renaissance

American Whiskey (+126%) and Irish Whiskey (+884%) have been standout performers, driven by craft distilling, premiumization, and changing consumer preferences toward brown spirits.

4. Rum Decline

Rum peaked in 2013 at 25.6M cases and has since declined 19%. The category has lost significant share to Tequila and whiskey alternatives, suggesting a structural repositioning challenge.

5. Scotch Weakness

Scotch has declined 16% overall (9.3M to 7.9M), struggling against domestic whiskey alternatives. The category faces ongoing pressure from American and Irish whiskey competition.

Methodology

Two scenarios were developed to bracket the range of plausible outcomes:

Aggressive Scenario

"The Decline Continues"

  • Extrapolates 2021-2024 trends forward
  • 10% annual dampening applied
  • Assumes post-pandemic decline is structural
  • Total market: 216M cases by 2029 (-5%)

Dampened Scenario

"Mean Reversion"

  • Blends recent and long-term trends
  • Caps annual change at +5%/-3%
  • Assumes 2022-24 was cyclical correction
  • Total market: 230M cases by 2029 (+1%)

7. Strategic Implications

Growth Opportunities

  • Tequila/Mezcal: Continue investment in premium/super-premium tiers. Category has runway for 20-30% growth through 2029.
  • American Whiskey: Focus on premiumization and craft positioning. Volume growth slowing but value growth remains strong.
  • Gin: Modest but stable growth opportunity, particularly in premium craft segment.

Categories Requiring Repositioning

  • Rum: Needs innovation and premiumization strategy. Current trajectory shows continued share loss.
  • Brandy/Cognac: Post-pandemic correction ongoing. Focus on core consumer retention.
  • Scotch: Faces structural headwinds from domestic whiskey. May need repositioning around heritage/luxury.

Planning Recommendation

Use the forecast range as a planning envelope rather than picking a single point estimate. Reality will likely fall between the Aggressive and Dampened scenarios. Build flexibility into supply chain and portfolio strategies to adapt to either outcome.

Key Uncertainties

  • Economic conditions (recession risk, consumer spending)
  • Regulatory changes (tariffs, taxes, advertising restrictions)
  • Cultural shifts (health trends, moderation movements)
  • Competitive dynamics (RTD growth, cannabis substitution)
